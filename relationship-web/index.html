Ammonia's Group

A loose, familiar circle

Ammonia at the Center

Ammonia's group is an informal social circle rather than a rigid organization. Its members have their own lives, interests, work, and relationships; they can come and go on their own terms.

Ammonia, Benoit, Riot, and Doll are among the people most often present. The wider circle includes Mold, Midge, Nadia, Cheche, and others whose lives intersect through friendship, shared spaces, and the people they know.

Ammonia Benoit Riot Doll Mold Midge Nadia Cheche

Benoit & Bell

Benoit and Bell are very good friends. Their friendship began when Bell was a young adult working hard-labor jobs in Nine to support herself through college, long before she built her career as a high-end hairstylist.

They have a history that predates Bell's polished professional life and remain connected beyond their different day-to-day worlds.

Doll & Mold

Doll first met Mold in a dirty alleyway a few years ago. Her immediate reaction was: “Disgusting. I fucking love it.”

Their friendship grew naturally from there. Doll genuinely enjoys Mold's filthy, strange, disgusting nature rather than merely putting up with it. Mold is accepted as herself, without needing to become more conventional.

Midge & the Group

Midge is deeply loved by the people around her. Her kindness is well known throughout Saint Jude's Landing, where shopkeepers and ordinary residents often recognize her and greet her by name.

Her reputation comes from years of everyday kindness rather than fame. She is an important presence in the group's social world.

Pike & the Social Circle

Pike belongs to Ammonia's group while maintaining a large independent network through the underground music and nightlife scene. He is comfortable around different people and can move easily between the group and his wider circle of musicians, performers, venue staff, and regulars.

Connections Across the City

Members of the group have different routines and social worlds. Some work in Nine, some spend time in the Hollows, and some travel regularly between Pugad and SJL. Shared membership creates familiarity, not a requirement to be together all the time.

Tansy & Pike

Best friends

A Longstanding Friendship

Tansy and Pike are best friends. Pike's relaxed, socially confident nature complements Tansy's more introspective and emotionally perceptive way of moving through the world. Their friendship is familiar and established, not a connection that needs to be constantly explained or made into a spectacle.

Pike works at Saint Jude's Landing's most popular underground club concert venue and regularly gets Tansy tickets to GOOD TEETH shows. Their friendship is one of the ways Tansy's love of music intersects with the city's wider underground scene.

Tansy & Riot

Tansy and Riot are close friends. Tansy accepts Riot's unusual behavior without constantly explaining or correcting him, which makes Riot unusually comfortable around him.

Tansy can recognize the person beneath Riot's relentless energy without turning their friendship into a project to fix him.

Pike's Wider Network

Pike's work gives him an extensive network of musicians, performers, bartenders, promoters, venue workers, and nightlife regulars. He is a connective point in SJL's underground music scene, with relationships that extend well beyond his closest friends.

GOOD TEETH

Five bandmates

A Collaborative Band

GOOD TEETH consists of five musicians whose different approaches collide to create the band's distinctive sound. Their working relationship is collaborative: arrangements develop through experimentation, reactions, disagreements, and ideas passed between members.

The band is not organized around one member being the sole creative force. Each musician contributes something distinct, and the music changes through their interaction.

Ives Vocals · 26
Renga Synth / Keyboard · 24
Tōgo Electric Guitar · 22
Purl Drums · 21
Riley Bass · 20

GOOD TEETH's music ranges from catchy electronic hooks and playful production to technical guitar, emo, post-hardcore, vulnerable melodies, and full-force hardcore. No single member owns one genre or emotional role.

Individual Band Connections

Established details

Ives & the Band

Ives is GOOD TEETH's lead singer. His intense, expressive performance style is one part of the band's broader collaborative identity, not a substitute for the other members' musical contributions.

Renga & the Band

Renga handles synth and keyboard, building electronic architecture, unusual melodies, digital textures, loops, and atmospheric layers into the band's sound.

Tōgo & the Band

Tōgo plays electric guitar, contributing atmospheric and technical riffs, layered harmonics, melodic passages, heavy riffs, and sudden bursts of intensity.

Purl & the Band

Purl's drumming drives the band's physical energy, reinforces electronic rhythms, supports vulnerable sections, and helps carry songs into their heavier passages.

Riley & the Band

Riley's bass provides emotional and harmonic gravity. His parts interact closely with Purl's drumming and can bring a melancholy undertone to music that otherwise sounds bright or playful.

Pike & GOOD TEETH

Pike's connection to the band comes through his work in SJL's underground music scene and his friendship with Tansy, whom he regularly gets tickets to GOOD TEETH shows.

Connections Across Places

Home, work & community

Cheche & Pugad

Cheche is closely connected to her family and community in Pugad, an independent rural community outside Saint Jude's Landing. She visits SJL frequently, but does not consider the city her home.

Her life connects the two communities through travel, friendships, skateboarding, and the local styles she brings back to Pugad.

Benoit & Vaudreaux

Benoit grew up in Vaudreaux before settling in Nine. His Cajun-Creole background, regional food, speech, and familiarity with waterways remain ordinary parts of his identity in SJL.

Bell & Nine

Bell's friendship with Benoit reaches back to her time working hard-labor jobs in Nine while supporting herself through college. Her current career and polished lifestyle developed later; her history with Nine is part of her own story.

Pike & Sivai

Pike grew up in Sivai, an island community in Orumai. Although his adult work and social life are now rooted in Saint Jude's Landing, his connection to his hometown remains part of his background.

How to Read the Web

Friendship Isn't Uniform

A group can contain best friends, close friends, casual acquaintances, and people who get along without spending every day together. Shared connections do not automatically create equal closeness.

People Have Separate Lives

Workplaces, music venues, neighborhoods, hometowns, and existing friendships connect characters naturally. Those connections can overlap without making every character part of every event.

A Living Network

Saint Jude's Landing is a city, not a closed cast list. Characters have routines, coworkers, neighbors, relatives, favorite places, and people they know from entirely different parts of their lives. Some connections are longstanding and intimate; others are as simple as recognizing someone at a venue or knowing who can get you through the door.
