# CREATOR
### An original fictional universe by Wren the Sage.

Creator is an original fictional universe built around an alien world full of strange ecosystems, sprawling cities, unfamiliar cultures, demi-humans, monsters, and people living lives of their own.

It's a world of contradictions: beautiful and filthy, affectionate and cruel, mundane and inexplicable. Ancient places exist alongside modern technology. Civilization grows around vast wildernesses, and the strange is simply another part of everyday life.

Creator is an ongoing independent creative project encompassing original worldbuilding, locations, characters, relationships, writing, and visual development.

**This is my world. These are my characters. This is my work.**

---

## ABOUT THE PROJECT

Creator is a living fictional setting developed through interconnected locations, communities, characters, and their relationships.

Its world includes places such as **Saint Jude's Landing**, a sprawling city where humans, demi-humans, and monsters share crowded streets, industrial districts, nightlife, neighborhoods, and the ordinary complications of being alive.

Every location has its own identity. Every character has a life beyond the moment they're introduced. The setting is intended to feel inhabited rather than arranged around a single storyline.

The project continues to grow as I develop its world, expand its cast, and explore the relationships between its people and places.
