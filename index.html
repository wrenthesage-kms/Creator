01 / THE BIG PICTURE

A WORLD THAT
refuses to sit still.

Creator is a vast, intensely lush alien planet inhabited by humans, demi-humans, and countless other beings.

Its geography is radically unfamiliar. Fragmented landmasses, enormous island chains, sprawling inland seas, branching waterways, and immense mountain systems form a world unlike anything found on Earth.

Emerald forests grow alongside modern cities. Strange rivers cut through developed regions. Wild ecosystems flourish beside transportation networks, industrial districts, farmland, and isolated settlements.

Creator is colorful, strange, expansive, and unmistakably alive.

05 / BEFORE THE NAMES WE KNOW

Why "Creator"?

The planet's name dates back to the first conscious life ever to inhabit it. They were the first beings capable of looking upon the world, understanding that they lived upon it, and giving it a name.

They believed that some long-dead divine being beneath the world nourished its extraordinary abundance of life. They called it Creator.

The name survived every civilization that followed. Eventually, nobody thought of Creator as a name chosen by someone anymore. It was simply the name of the world.
