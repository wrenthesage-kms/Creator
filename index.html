First things first

Welcome to a world
that doesn't sit still.

Creator is a vast, intensely lush alien planet inhabited by humans, demi-humans, and countless other beings.

There are no familiar Earth-shaped continents. Instead, enormous island chains, fragmented landmasses, inland seas, branching waterways, and mountain systems stretch across a world that has never looked anything like ours.

Strange forests grow near modern cities. Rivers cut through developed regions. Industrial neighborhoods exist alongside unfamiliar wildlife, and people build ordinary lives in places that would look impossible anywhere else.

One last thing

Why Creator?

The first beings capable of understanding that they lived upon a world believed that a long-dead divine being beneath it nourished its extraordinary abundance of life.

They named the planet Creator. The name survived every civilization that followed, until it became simply what the world was called.
