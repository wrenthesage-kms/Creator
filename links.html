MY SCHEDULE (I DON'T HAVE ONE)

I work in hyperfixation cycles. Sometimes I spend days obsessing over bots and drop five at once. Sometimes I go quiet for a few days because I'm busy tweaking, rebuilding, and obsessively improving the next batch. Then suddenly, surprise! More bots.

That's the workflow. No posting calendar, no guaranteed release days, no neat little timetable. Just bursts of creation, periods of tinkering, and whatever my brain decides we're doing this week.

Have a request? I work through requests within a reasonable amount of time. You don't need to worry that your idea has disappeared into the void just because I'm not posting every day.

If I'm quiet, I'm probably still making things. I'm just not always making things you can see yet. ♡
