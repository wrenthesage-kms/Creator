Cultural Communities

Established communities with distinct cultural influences, local traditions, everyday routines, and identities of their own.

Creator's cultural communities are established places shaped by their own histories, languages, traditions, businesses, and everyday lives. Their cultural influences inform local identity without making every community identical. Some are dense urban settlements; others have distinct environments and relationships to the wider world.

Community directory

Kurobane

Major community · Japanese influence

A dense modern community with deep local history, traditional architecture, gardens, family businesses, restaurants, markets, nightlife, and strong craft traditions.

Cultural influence Japanese
Languages Japanese and English

Muwon

Major community · Korean influence

A dense urban community with apartment neighborhoods, markets, restaurants, family businesses, nightlife, music, fashion, and long-established local traditions.

Cultural influence Korean
Languages Korean and English

Xolapa

Major community · Mexican influence

A colorful established community containing dense residential neighborhoods, plazas, markets, restaurants, family businesses, music, celebrations, and older architecture.

Cultural influence Mexican
Languages Spanish and English

Pugad

Independent rural community · Filipino influence

An established community outside Saint Jude’s Landing, surrounded by alien wilderness, with local homes, businesses, schools, farms, family networks, and its own strong community identity.

Cultural influence Filipino
Languages Tagalog and English
Relationship to Saint Jude’s Landing Closely connected to SJL while remaining culturally and socially independent.

Kaiwana

Deep-water community · Orumai · Hawaiian influence

An established deep-water community built among enormous reefs, volcanic formations, submerged valleys, and island chains far from Orumai’s major surface settlements. Homes, businesses, gathering spaces, markets, schools, and family communities are integrated directly into the surrounding ocean environment.

Region Orumai
Cultural influence Hawaiian
Languages Hawaiian and English
Community type Established deep-water settlement
Environment Warm deep waters, towering reefs, volcanic rock, enormous kelp-like vegetation, glowing marine life, underwater caves, strong currents, and unusually diverse aquatic ecosystems.
Culture Strong traditions surrounding ocean stewardship, navigation, voyaging, fishing, canoe culture, music, dance, family, food, storytelling, and relationships between people and the sea.

✧ Distinct Places, Distinct Lives

These communities have their own cultural influences and local identities. Pugad remains an independent rural community outside Saint Jude’s Landing. Kaiwana belongs to the aquatic region of Orumai and is defined by its deep-water environment as well as its Hawaiian cultural influence.
