Creator's aquatic regions encompass enormous interconnected waters, branching river systems, wetlands, islands, coastal areas, and established communities shaped by their surroundings. These are inhabited regions with their own towns, cities, businesses, cultures, and connections to the wider world—not empty wilderness between settlements.

Major aquatic regions

Orumai

Major aquatic region

Orumai is a vast interconnected aquatic region containing enormous inland waters, river systems, wetlands, islands, coastal areas, and dense aquatic communities.

❧ Environment

Intensely lush and humid, with strange aquatic vegetation, enormous waterways, colorful wildlife, and frequent storms.

⌂ Civilization

Numerous established towns, cities, fishing communities, ports, islands, and waterfront settlements exist throughout Orumai.

↔ Relationship to Saint Jude’s Landing

Orumai has strong trade, travel, family, cultural, and entertainment connections with Saint Jude’s Landing.

Kaluwa

Major aquatic region

Kaluwa is an enormous freshwater basin containing sprawling lakes, branching rivers, marshes, islands, and communities built directly around the water.

❧ Environment

Dense vegetation, warm humid weather, colorful freshwater ecosystems, enormous aquatic wildlife, and unusual waterways.

⌂ Civilization

Kaluwa contains old established communities, busy ports, rural settlements, floating structures, farms, and modern cities.

♫ Culture

Kaluwa has its own regional traditions, food, music, businesses, and local identities rather than functioning as one uniform culture.

↔ Relationship to Saint Jude’s Landing

Kaluwa is closely connected to Saint Jude’s Landing through transportation, commerce, employment, tourism, and family networks.

Aquatic communities

Established settlements throughout Orumai and Kaluwa, each with its own relationship to the surrounding water.

Tavira

Waterfront city · Orumai

A large, busy aquatic city built around several connected waterways, with dense neighborhoods, docks, markets, restaurants, and ferry routes.

Mabiko

Fishing and river community · Kaluwa

An older community surrounded by wetlands and branching rivers, known for fishing, food production, local markets, and family businesses.

Sivai

Island community · Orumai

A compact island settlement with dense housing, small businesses, boat transportation, and a strong local identity.

Talua

Wetland community · Kaluwa

A low-lying community connected by raised walkways, small bridges, boats, and narrow waterways.

Kaiwana

Deep-water community · Orumai

An established deep-water community built around enormous submerged formations, deep reefs, underwater valleys, and interconnected marine settlements. Dense residential areas, businesses, gathering spaces, markets, schools, and transportation routes are integrated directly into the surrounding deep-water environment.
