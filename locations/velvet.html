Luxury exists alongside exploitation, crime, addiction, trafficking, blackmail, organized crime, predatory businesses, and people willing to take advantage of vulnerable patrons or workers. Not every establishment is safe simply because it is expensive.

♫ The Performers

Musicians, dancers, actors, drag performers, burlesque artists, comedians, escorts, adult performers, and other entertainers work throughout Velvet. Many performers develop dedicated local followings.

♡ The Complications

Velvet is a place where people meet strangers, lovers, business partners, enemies, future spouses, temporary hookups, lifelong friends, and people they immediately regret meeting. Relationships can begin casually and become complicated very quickly.

⌂ The Streetscape

Velvet contains a mixture of old buildings, renovated venues, apartments, hotels, theaters, restaurants, storefronts, private residences, and purpose-built entertainment spaces. The district’s buildings vary enormously rather than following one unified architectural style.
