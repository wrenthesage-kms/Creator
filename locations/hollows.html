Saint Jude’s Landing · District dossier

The Hollows

Old streets, overgrown buildings, and quiet places where the city becomes a little stranger than it ought to be.

City district Overgrown Fog & waterways Day and night
❧ · · · ☾ · · · ❧

The Hollows is one of the oldest, strangest, and most isolated parts of Saint Jude’s Landing. Old buildings, narrow streets, neglected infrastructure, dense vegetation, and perpetual dampness give the district a character all its own. In some places, the city seems to grow quiet in ways that are difficult to explain.

The district

❧ Environment

Alien trees, vines, moss, fungi, flowers, and other vegetation grow through buildings, across rooftops, along streets, and around abandoned structures. Fog frequently settles throughout the Hollows, especially at night and around its waterways.

⌂ Architecture

Buildings are old, irregular, and heavily modified over generations. Some are beautifully maintained homes or businesses; others are collapsing structures swallowed by vegetation. Old construction sits directly beside newer additions, creating neighborhoods that feel layered rather than planned.

♧ Population

Humans, demi-humans, monsters, and other beings live throughout the Hollows. The district attracts people and species who prefer privacy, isolation, unusual communities, or lifestyles that might draw more attention elsewhere.

☾ Monsters

The Hollows has an unusually high population of strange, elusive, nocturnal, and heavily nonhuman creatures. Some residents have unusual sensory abilities, camouflage, bioluminescence, predatory anatomy, or bodies adapted to the district’s damp and overgrown environment.

♡ Attraction

Human/monster and interspecies relationships are common in the Hollows. Its residents tend to be less concerned with conventional ideas of beauty or appropriate pairings. Strange anatomy, frightening appearances, extreme size differences, fangs, claws, tails, fur, scales, and other monstrous traits can all be objects of genuine romantic or sexual attraction.

✧ · · · ☘ · · · ✧

Private lives & public dangers

♧ Adult Life

The Hollows has intimate bars, private clubs, underground parties, fetish communities, brothels, secluded homes, and other adult spaces hidden throughout the district. Privacy is highly valued, and people often know exactly where to go when they do not want to be seen.

⚠ Danger

The Hollows has a reputation for disappearances, stalking, violence, predatory creatures, criminal activity, and unexplained incidents. Some stories are exaggerated. Others are not. Residents learn quickly which streets, buildings, waterways, and creatures should be avoided.

⌂ Community

Despite its reputation, the Hollows is not simply a dangerous wasteland. People have homes, families, jobs, relationships, favorite restaurants, local businesses, and longstanding friendships. Neighbors often know one another extremely well.

☾ After Dark

The district changes dramatically after dark. Fog becomes thicker, vegetation becomes louder with nocturnal life, strange lights appear among the trees, and creatures that remain hidden during the day become active. People who know the Hollows understand that nighttime does not necessarily mean the same thing as daytime here.

Character & atmosphere

✧ Atmosphere

Damp, overgrown, intimate, eerie, beautiful, secluded, sensual, strange, and occasionally terrifying.

❧ Tone

Gothic without being a fixed aesthetic. The Hollows can be romantic, filthy, cozy, erotic, funny, violent, horrifying, or completely mundane depending on where someone is and who they encounter.
