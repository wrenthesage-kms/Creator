This is a growing directory of businesses around Saint Jude’s Landing: the independent shops, everyday essentials, local hangouts, and neighborhood institutions that give people somewhere to go. NO. 004 / THE REGULARS

MILEWEL

♨
Dive Bar

Where: Saint Jude’s Landing

A small, filthy neighborhood bar with cheap drinks, old furniture, regular customers, questionable bathrooms, and unexpectedly good food. Not pretty. Not trying to be pretty. Still somehow everybody's favorite.

The local reputation

Beloved by locals despite looking like it should have been condemned years ago. People have opinions about this place, and most of them are strangely affectionate.

NO. 005 / TURN IT UP

SIDE A

♫
Live-Music Venue

Where: Saint Jude’s Landing

A popular underground venue hosting local bands, touring musicians, experimental performers, and small shows. The kind of place where you go for the music and come away knowing somebody's entire complicated friend group.

The atmosphere

Crowded, sweaty, loud, intimate, and constantly changing depending on who's playing.

The social scene

Musicians, bartenders, promoters, venue workers, regulars, and fans overlap heavily here.

A city is made of places people remember.

The best-known landmarks aren't the only places that matter. A convenience store can be part of somebody's daily routine. A dive bar can become a second home. A repair shop can keep a neighborhood running. A music venue can bring people together who otherwise might never meet. These businesses aren't just scenery; they're part of the lives happening around them. This directory can grow as more local places find their way onto the map.
