02 / Outside the city

Across Creator

Established communities shaped by their surrounding environments.

Communities grow around the places where people can make a life. Terrain, weather, wildlife, and local resources all influence how a settlement develops, without defining every person who lives there.

CALDERA

Hot-spring community

An established settlement built around naturally occurring hot springs surrounded by dense alien vegetation.

Local culture: Bathing, hospitality, food, tourism, and local businesses developed around the springs without making the entire community a resort town.

KARSAI

Mountain community

An established settlement built into a massive mountain system.

Built for the terrain: Homes, businesses, roads, stairs, bridges, and transportation infrastructure are adapted to the steep terrain.

LOWMARSH

Wetland community

An old settlement surrounded by wetlands, dense vegetation, shallow waterways, raised roads, bridges, and unusual wildlife.

Local reputation: Locals are accustomed to conditions that would make outsiders deeply uncomfortable.
