Creator isn't Earth with a different coat of paint. Its geography, ecosystems, and communities have their own histories. Humans live alongside demi-humans, monsters, and all kinds of other beings. Some places are enormous urban sprawls; others are independent communities surrounded by alien wilderness. There isn't one universal way to live here. Where you are matters. Who lives there matters. And the place itself has a personality, too.

Pugad

An established rural community surrounded by alien wilderness. Homes, farms, schools, local businesses, and family networks form the backbone of everyday life. Pugad has its own strong identity, alongside close cultural and social connections with Saint Jude’s Landing.

≈ Southern region

Vaudreaux

Warm, humid country threaded with branching waterways, flooded forests, wetlands, and sprawling bayou-like landscapes. Cajun and Creole cultural influences shape local food, music, language, gatherings, and the rhythms of everyday life.

✳ A world is more than its biggest city.

Saint Jude’s Landing might be a major urban center, but it doesn't own the rest of Creator. Independent communities and distant regions have their own histories, cultures, environments, and ways of life. A community's connection to the city doesn't make it part of the city. Geography matters, but so do the people who live somewhere, the things they build, the things they remember, and the things they absolutely refuse to put up with.
