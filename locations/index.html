LOCATIONS

A world of sprawling cities, old communities, strange wilderness, crowded neighborhoods, and places that have been here long enough to collect a few stories of their own.

✳ ✦ ❋ ✦ ✳

Creator is enormous, colorful, and unmistakably alive. Its geography bears little resemblance to Earth's. Communities have grown around its waterways, mountains, wetlands, coastlines, and extraordinary ecosystems. Modern cities and remote settlements alike are home to humans, demi-humans, monsters, and countless other beings. Every place has its own character, its own people, and its own way of life.

✧ A note on the map

Creator is a world, not a collection of neighborhoods arranged around one city. Saint Jude’s Landing is a major urban center, but independent communities and distant regions have their own histories, cultures, environments, and ways of life. A connection to the city does not make a community part of it, and a place's character comes from the people who live there as much as from its geography.
