Pugad is an established rural community on Creator, surrounded by lush alien wilderness. Its homes, local businesses, self-sufficient infrastructure, and generations of residents give it a strong local identity. It is quieter and less densely developed than Saint Jude’s Landing, but it remains firmly connected to the modern world.

INDEPENDENT COMMUNITY · OUTSIDE SAINT JUDE’S LANDING · NOT A DISTRICT

Life in Pugad

❧ Environment

Pugad is rural, lush, open, and surrounded by alien vegetation and wildlife. Life here is quieter than in Saint Jude’s Landing, with an established, practical, lived-in character shaped by the community and its surroundings.

⌂ Community

Families, long-term residents, local businesses, schools, teenagers, daily routines, and generational familiarity shape everyday life. Residents share a strong sense of local identity built over generations.

⚒ Self-Sufficiency

Pugad has established, self-sufficient infrastructure alongside homes and local businesses. It functions as a community in its own right, rather than depending on its identity or existence upon Saint Jude’s Landing.

♧ Rural Life

Less densely developed and less industrially dense than Saint Jude’s Landing, Pugad has more open space and a quieter pace of life. Its rural character exists alongside the practical routines and established infrastructure of a modern community.

✧ · · · ☘ · · · ✧

Connected, not absorbed

↔ Relationship to Saint Jude’s Landing

Pugad is physically outside Saint Jude’s Landing but culturally connected to it. Younger residents travel into the city and bring back music, fashion, food, technology, gossip, trends, and niche interests. The city influences local life without replacing Pugad’s own identity.

✦ Local Identity

Pugad is its own community, with its own residents, routines, businesses, and generational connections. It is not an extension of Saint Jude’s Landing and is not one of the city’s districts.

♡ Connection

Cheche is associated with Pugad.

☘ Pugad, in a Nutshell

Rural, lush, open, practical, established, self-sufficient, and lived-in. Pugad is quieter than Saint Jude’s Landing, surrounded by Creator’s alien wilderness, and shaped by generations of community life. It belongs to the same modern civilization while remaining unmistakably its own place.
