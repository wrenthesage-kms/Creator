A place with roots

Pugad is an established rural community on Creator, surrounded by lush alien wilderness. Homes, local businesses, self-sufficient infrastructure, and generations of residents give it a strong local identity.

It is quieter and less densely developed than Saint Jude’s Landing, but it isn't cut off from the modern world. Pugad has its own routines and community life, shaped by the people who live there and the landscape beyond their doors.

✿

Not the city. Not a suburb of the city. Not waiting to become the city.

01 /

The shape of everyday life

❋

Alien by nature

Pugad is rural, lush, open, and surrounded by alien vegetation and wildlife. Its quieter pace and practical, lived-in character are shaped by the community and the environment around it.

⌂

Everybody has history

Families, long-term residents, local businesses, schools, teenagers, and daily routines shape the community. Generational familiarity gives Pugad a strong sense of local identity.

⚒

Built to keep going

Established, self-sufficient infrastructure exists alongside homes and local businesses. Pugad functions as a community in its own right, not as a place whose existence depends on the city.

♧

Room to breathe

Pugad is less densely developed and less industrially dense than Saint Jude’s Landing. Open space and a quieter pace coexist with the practical routines and infrastructure of modern life.

✳

Modern doesn't mean metropolitan.

Pugad belongs to the same modern civilization as Saint Jude’s Landing. It has established infrastructure, local businesses, schools, and connections to the wider world. Its rural character isn't a sign that time stopped at the edge of town; it's simply a different way of living.

02 /

Connected, not absorbed

City influence, Pugad identity.

Pugad is physically outside Saint Jude’s Landing but culturally connected to it. Younger residents travel into the city and bring back music, fashion, food, technology, gossip, trends, and niche interests. Those influences reach the community without replacing what makes Pugad its own place.

The important distinction

Nearby ≠ part of it.

Pugad has its own residents, routines, businesses, and generational connections. It is not an extension of Saint Jude’s Landing and is not one of the city's districts.

♡

Local connection: Cheche is associated with Pugad.

03 / The short version

A whole life beyond the city limits.

Rural, lush, open, practical, established, self-sufficient, and lived-in. Pugad is surrounded by Creator’s alien wilderness and shaped by generations of community life. It belongs to the same modern civilization as Saint Jude’s Landing while remaining unmistakably its own place.
