General

Saint Jude’s Landing is an enormous, sprawling modern alien city where humans, demi-humans, monsters, and countless other species live together. It is beautiful, filthy, colorful, crowded, dangerous, affectionate, horny, violent, mundane, and strange all at once.

Society

Humans, demi-humans, and monsters are ordinary members of society. Species, body types, ages, appearances, disabilities, gender expressions, and relationship dynamics vary wildly. Nonhuman beings are not inherently categorized as animals or beasts; many are fully sapient people with their own cultures, personalities, desires, families, and communities.

Attraction

Attraction between humans, demi-humans, and monstrous species is commonplace. Human/monster relationships, interspecies dating, marriage, hookups, families, and long-term partnerships are all established parts of city life. Some species are considered especially attractive or desirable by particular subcultures, while others are feared, fetishized, misunderstood, or considered unconventional.

Monsterfucking

Sexual and romantic attraction toward monstrous beings is a normal part of the city’s adult culture. Fangs, claws, tails, horns, fur, scales, unusual anatomy, enormous size differences, strange biology, and other nonhuman traits can be objects of attraction. Human/monster relationships can be sweet, casual, romantic, dysfunctional, obsessive, kinky, or complicated depending on the individuals involved.

Relationships

Saint Jude’s Landing contains conventional couples, unconventional couples, open relationships, possessive relationships, toxic relationships, dominant/submissive dynamics, age-gap relationships between consenting adults, interspecies relationships, and relationships that outsiders may find bizarre. The city does not have one universal attitude toward relationships.

Creatures

Monsters range from gentle and beautiful to grotesque, dangerous, predatory, enormous, or deeply strange. Some can pass as nearly human; others cannot. Many live ordinary lives alongside people who would once have considered them monsters.

People

Pretty boys, femboys, short men, fat men, muscular women, plus-size women, dad-bod men, older adults, scarred people, disabled people, unconventional-looking people, and monstrous or heavily nonhuman individuals are all ordinary parts of the population.

Adult Life

Dating, sex, nightlife, kink communities, fetish subcultures, clubs, bars, brothels, pornography, prostitution, and unconventional relationships are established parts of adult city life. Some establishments specifically cater to interspecies or monster-oriented communities.

Underworld

Crime, organized crime, theft, violence, trafficking, black markets, corruption, gangs, illegal businesses, and dangerous individuals exist throughout the city.

Darkness

Horrific events, abuse, murder, disappearances, exploitation, obsession, stalking, manipulation, domestic violence, predatory individuals, and other extreme circumstances can exist alongside ordinary life. Not every monster is dangerous, and not every human is safe.

Subcultures

The city contains countless niche communities, underground scenes, fandoms, fashion movements, fetish communities, music scenes, creature communities, alternative lifestyles, and strange local traditions.

City Life

Restaurants, apartments, schools, hospitals, workplaces, shops, markets, transit stations, bars, clubs, theaters, arcades, parks, clinics, warehouses, factories, residential streets, and entertainment districts fill the city. Ordinary errands and mundane personal problems happen alongside bizarre and dangerous situations.

Nature

Creator’s alien ecosystems grow directly into Saint Jude’s Landing. Dense vegetation, strange flowers, enormous trees, waterways, alien wildlife, and wild growth can appear between buildings or consume abandoned structures.

Technology

Modern technology is widespread and practical. Technology does not define the city’s aesthetic or create a cyberpunk society.

Atmosphere

Colorful, grimy, lush, crowded, sexually open, socially chaotic, dangerous, funny, beautiful, and deeply lived-in.

Tone

Adult, strange, irreverent, romantic, erotic, violent, messy, occasionally horrific, frequently funny, and grounded in everyday life.
