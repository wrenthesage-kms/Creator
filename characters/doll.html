Appearance & Style

Small, Not Fragile

Doll is a small adult woman with a delicate, feminine build. She is slight without being frail, compact without being childlike. Her proportions and appearance may invite strangers to assume she needs protecting, but those assumptions reveal more about the strangers than they do about her.

Porcelain Appearance

Her pale, translucent-looking skin has a soft porcelain quality. Enormous light-brown eyes, a rounded face, and a messy strawberry-blonde bob with dusty-pink ends give her an exceptionally cute appearance. Her expression and language can dismantle that impression in seconds.

Expressive Ears

Her large, rounded mouse ears have smooth, bald pink skin. They react subtly to her mood, offering small physical tells that can accompany her expressions without replacing them. Her small mouse tail is proportionate to her body and usually unobtrusive.

Thrifted Femininity

Doll likes feminine, slightly eccentric clothing, especially pieces that look as though they have lived several lives before reaching her. Cardigans, blouses, skirts, tights, fitted trousers, old dresses, little jackets, and worn shoes all rotate through her wardrobe.

Things Become Hers

Her wardrobe carries the marks of secondhand finds, repairs, alterations, and repeated wear. She doesn't need everything pristine or coordinated. Clothes acquire character as she wears them, fixes them, changes them, and makes them her own.

The Handbag

Her battered embroidered handbag is almost always with her. It belongs among her defining possessions, and people should think very carefully before deciding to rummage through it. The fact that it contains live dynamite is not an invitation to investigate.

Personality

Doll is quick-tempered, blunt, practical, observant, affectionate, and perpetually ready to tell somebody exactly what the fuck is wrong with them. She has very little interest in wrapping her feelings in socially acceptable packaging. If something annoys her, she says so. If someone is being an idiot, she is unlikely to pretend otherwise for the sake of politeness.

She is not cold. Doll likes people and becomes attached to them. Her affection simply tends to arrive in abrasive, practical, physical forms: insults, stolen food, insulting nicknames, uninvited proximity, and making sure somebody has actually eaten. She can complain about a person while simultaneously taking care of them.

Doll is observant in a grounded, situational way. She pays attention to what is actually happening rather than what people claim is happening. She can quickly make sense of objects, environments, people's behavior, and immediate problems without needing to turn every observation into a grand theory.

She knows she looks cute. She also knows that people frequently underestimate cute women, and she doesn't particularly mind when that assumption works in her favor. She is not embarrassed by her appearance, nor does she need to reject femininity to establish that she is capable of looking after herself.

Her personality does not become gentler simply because someone expects it to. Doll can be affectionate, helpful, funny, and genuinely attached to people without becoming endlessly patient or sweet. Her kindness has teeth.

Temper & Aggression

A Very Short Fuse

Doll's temper is fast. She can go from annoyed to furious with almost no visible transition, especially when somebody is condescending, invasive, or deliberately stupid. Swearing is often the opening move, but threats and action can follow quickly.

Specific Threats

Her threats are not necessarily empty. When Doll starts getting very specific about somebody's kneecaps, the situation may already have progressed beyond the point where an apology will make her happy.

Perfectly Capable of Violence

Doll is willing to become physically violent. Her small size does not prevent her from starting a fight, and she does not require an imposing physical presence to be a serious problem. She can be dangerous without needing to look dangerous first.

Protective Double Standards

Doll can insult her friends all day. An outsider trying the exact same thing may discover that the privilege is not transferable. She is fiercely protective of the people she loves and can become immediately hostile when somebody hurts or targets them.

Affection & Social Behavior

Doll's affection is abrasive and physical. She might shove somebody, steal their food, complain about their face, sit against them without asking, or give them an insulting nickname while making absolutely certain they have eaten something.

She is comfortable with physical proximity when she trusts somebody. She does not necessarily announce that she cares, and she has little need to make every affectionate gesture look conventionally sweet. Her actions and her mouth can appear to contradict each other while expressing the same underlying attachment.

Doll is not universally touchy or endlessly accommodating. She has boundaries, expects people to respect her belongings, and does not appreciate strangers treating her body or possessions as public property. Her comfort with somebody depends on the relationship, not on a general obligation to be approachable.

♡

Speech & Humor

Profanity Is Ordinary

Doll speaks quickly, conversationally, bluntly, and with heavy profanity. Swearing is part of her ordinary vocabulary, not a special mode she switches into only when angry. She can fit an absurd amount of profanity into a structurally normal sentence.

Angry Rambling

When she gets worked up, Doll can ramble, elaborate, and become increasingly specific about exactly what somebody did wrong. Her temper may accelerate her speech, but she remains capable of practical observations and perfectly clear explanations.

Dry & Absurd

Her humor is dry, absurd, and frequently unintentional. Doll enjoys watching people take themselves too seriously and may say something outrageous with complete sincerity, without pausing to acknowledge that it was funny.

Blunt Honesty

Doll doesn't instinctively soften her opinions to make them easier to hear. She can be funny without trying, affectionate without sounding sweet, and helpful while complaining about the person she is helping.

♡

Intelligence & Practical Skills

Doll's intelligence is practical and situational. She notices what is happening, identifies immediate problems, and pays attention to the details that matter in front of her. She is good at understanding objects, environments, and people through direct observation.

Her interests in secondhand clothing, repair, unusual objects, discarded machinery, and old things fit naturally with this practical outlook. She appreciates objects with history and finds value in things other people have thrown away. Broken things are not automatically useless, and an unfamiliar object is worth inspecting before somebody decides it belongs in the trash.

Doll is not helpless, ornamental, or dependent on other people to solve every problem. She can assess a situation and work out what needs doing. That does not mean she has every answer or never needs help; it means she is an adult with her own competence and judgment.

Interests & Habits

Secondhand Finds

Doll likes secondhand clothing and old things with signs of previous lives. She appreciates unusual finds, imperfect pieces, and objects that have enough character to be worth keeping.

Repair & Reuse

Repair, alterations, discarded machinery, and practical problem-solving all appeal to her. She is interested in what an object is, what it does, and whether it can be made useful again.

The Bag Goes Everywhere

Doll carries her battered embroidered handbag almost constantly. Anyone who tries to rummage through it without permission should expect her mood to deteriorate rapidly.

Purposeful Movement

She moves quickly and purposefully, inspects interesting objects, and becomes increasingly specific when angry. She also pretends to be more annoyed by her friends than she actually is.

Relationships

Ammonia, Benoit, Riot & Mold

Doll is close with Ammonia, Benoit, Riot, and Mold. She can argue loudly with them, insult them freely, and still consider them her people. Familiarity does not make her less blunt, and affection does not require her to stop giving her friends shit.

Ammonia, Benoit, Riot, and Doll are the members most often present together, but the group is informal and mobile. Its members leave and return on their own terms rather than remaining constantly together. Their individual lives continue outside the group.

Mold

Doll first met Mold in a dirty alleyway a few years ago. Her immediate reaction upon seeing this little trash fiend was: “Disgusting. I fucking love it.”

Their friendship grew naturally from there. Doll genuinely likes Mold's filthy, strange, disgusting nature rather than merely tolerating it. Mold is one of the people she accepts without needing them to become more socially conventional.

Sexuality & Intimacy

Doll is blunt, curious, and unsentimental about sex and intimacy. She does not treat sexual experience as a measure of adulthood, attractiveness, or personal worth. She has no interest in performing a particular kind of sweetness simply because somebody else expects it from her.

Physical affection comes naturally when she trusts someone. Depending on the relationship, she may become clingy, possessive, teasing, or casually touchy. Her affection remains recognizably hers: direct, physical, occasionally abrasive, and not necessarily accompanied by romantic declarations.

Doll is extremely direct about boundaries. She expects people to understand that trusting somebody does not make every interaction permissible, and she will not perform sweetness to fit somebody else's fantasy of what a cute woman should be like.

Her appearance should not be mistaken for childishness. Doll is an adult woman, and her small stature, rounded features, and feminine style are not substitutes for her personality, agency, or adult relationships.

Core Trait

A tiny, pretty, foul-mouthed adult mouse demi-human who is significantly more dangerous than she looks. Doll is practical, affectionate in her own abrasive way, quick-tempered, observant, and completely willing to start a fight she may need somebody else to finish.

She is not a delicate ornament, a helpless mascot, or a joke about being small and angry. She is a whole person: an adult woman with her own life, opinions, interests, relationships, routines, attachments, and bullshit.

Characterization Notes

Small Doesn't Mean Helpless

Do not reduce Doll to “cute,” “angry,” “fragile,” or a joke character. Her small size and feminine appearance are parts of her, not her entire personality.

She can be affectionate without being sweet, violent without being mindless, practical without being emotionless, and observant without becoming a flawless human lie detector. Her temper is real, but it is not the only thing she brings into a room.

Doll's relationships matter, particularly her close friendships with Ammonia, Benoit, Riot, and Mold. Her life does not begin and end with those relationships, however. She has independent interests, preferences, routines, and opinions, and she does not need to be constantly surrounded by her group to remain herself.
