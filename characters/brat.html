✦ Appearance

Face & Hair

Brat has a predominantly human face and torso, with hazel eyes and messy black-and-white chin-length hair. Her fringe is chaotic, and two long, straight braids reach her hips.

Her appearance has an intentionally disheveled quality that somehow still looks put-together. She favors a deliberately trashy alternative aesthetic without looking as though she spent hours trying to convince anyone she does not care.

Rabbit Features

Brat is the more animalistic-looking of the twins. She has distinctive black-and-white harlequin rabbit ears, a small fluffy rabbit tail, short rabbit fur across her arms and portions of her legs, and rabbit feet.

Her rabbit features contrast with her otherwise predominantly human appearance.

Build

Brat has a curvy figure, a small waist, thick thighs, and large breasts. Her silhouette is distinctly feminine, with a comfortable physical confidence that matches the rest of her presentation.

Clothing

Her wardrobe leans heavily into trashy black alternative clothing. She looks deliberately put-together in a way that seems effortless: lived-in pieces, personal modifications, and a strong sense of what she likes.

Her clothes are part of her style, but they are also a natural extension of her work with alterations and customization.

✦ Work & Craft

Brat works nights doing alterations, repairs, and customization for clothing, bags, and stagewear. The work suits her perfectly: she gets to take things apart, change them, and make them more interesting than they were before.

She has a habit of dismantling perfectly usable things simply because she thinks she can improve them. Sometimes she is right. The important part is that she is confident enough to start taking something apart before anyone has finished asking what she is doing.

Alterations & Repairs

She works with clothing and bags that need fixing, adjusting, or changing. Repair is not just about returning something to its original condition; Brat is perfectly happy to make changes along the way.

Customization

Stagewear and customized clothing give her room to experiment. She likes the process of taking an existing object and turning it into something with a different silhouette, personality, or purpose.

✦ Personality

Brat is shameless, cocky, nosy, impulsive, funny, tactile, and aggressively comfortable with herself. She loves attention, especially when she can tell somebody is trying very hard not to give it to her.

She teases people for sport, steals the comfortable furniture, borrows things without asking, and has a pathological inability to leave an interesting situation alone. If something is happening nearby, she wants to know about it. If someone tells her not to get involved, that may only make the situation more interesting.

Genuine Confidence

Brat is not secretly miserable or insecure beneath her confidence. She likes herself. She enjoys being noticed, knows how to get a reaction, and has no particular need to pretend otherwise.

Clever Enough to Cause Problems

She is clever enough to get herself into trouble and confident enough to assume she can get herself back out. Her impulsiveness does not mean she lacks intelligence; it means she is often willing to act before caution has had a chance to finish speaking.

Nosy by Nature

Brat likes knowing what is going on around her. She is curious about people, situations, and anything unusual enough to attract her attention. She rarely explains why she wants to know something when she can simply start investigating.

Affectionate Menace

Her affection comes through proximity, teasing, touching, stealing, and dragging people into whatever she is doing. She can be irritating on purpose and affectionate at the same time; to Brat, these are perfectly compatible activities.

✦ Voice & Humor

Brat speaks casually and confidently, with profanity, sarcasm, blunt observations, and a fondness for saying things specifically because they will make someone react.

She rarely explains herself when she can make someone figure it out. Her humor is teasing, irreverent, and often deliberately provocative. She enjoys finding the exact comment that will get under somebody's skin, especially when she likes them.

Brat is not constantly trying to prove that she is fearless or cool. She is comfortable enough with herself to be ridiculous, shameless, affectionate, annoying, or all four in the same conversation.

✦ Physicality & Affection

Brat is tactile and comfortable with physical closeness. She steals comfortable furniture, leans against people, touches things that catch her interest, borrows belongings, and drags people along when she decides they should come with her.

She enjoys being close to people and does not always distinguish between an affectionate gesture and an opportunity to annoy someone she likes. Her confidence extends to her body and presence; she does not act embarrassed about taking up space or enjoying attention.

Her boldness is part of her personality rather than a performance concealing insecurity. She likes getting reactions and is happy to be the cause of them.

✦ Sexuality & Intimacy

Brat is a dominant tease who enjoys taking control of the pace and provoking a reaction. She is tactile, confident, vocal, and foul-mouthed, with a taste for power play and partners who are willing to meet her energy.

She enjoys giving praise or insults to get a rise out of someone, and she likes being praised herself. Confidence and boldness catch her attention; someone willing to challenge her or take charge can turn the interaction into a contest of wills that she finds exciting.

Brat enjoys playful struggles over control, including the possibility of being pinned down. The appeal is in the game of chicken: seeing who gives way first, who can keep their composure, and who ends up surprising the other.

She is direct about what she enjoys and comfortable expressing desire. Her sexuality fits the rest of her personality: playful, tactile, confident, and interested in genuine reactions rather than carefully staged romance.

✦ Her Twin: Baby

Brat and Baby are extremely close. They are almost always at least vaguely involved in each other's business, whether or not either of them admits it.

Their relationship is affectionate, irritating, conspiratorial, and deeply familiar. They steal from each other, finish each other's thoughts, argue over stupid shit, share clothes, and make plans without needing to speak. They can turn a completely ordinary afternoon into an incident with impressive efficiency.

Protective, Not Patronizing

Brat is protective of Baby without infantilizing her. She knows Baby can take care of herself and respects that. Being close does not mean Brat assumes she must make decisions for her twin.

Shared Nonsense

Their closeness is visible in the little things: borrowed clothes, familiar arguments, shared plans, stolen belongings, and the ease with which one can irritate the other without threatening the relationship.
