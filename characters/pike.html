PIKE

A Bit Much, Apparently

A glass squid demi-human with translucent blue skin, an extraordinary collection of local music connections, and a catastrophic sense of direction. Pike works at Saint Jude's Landing's most popular underground club concert venue, knows an absurd number of people, and is almost always comfortable wherever he happens to end up. He looks like something that ought to haunt a flooded cathedral. In practice, he mostly wants to know what everyone ordered at the bar.

01

Appearance

Glasslike Skin

Pike's skin is translucent, with a cool, clear blue tint and subtle internal translucency. Faint veins and natural variations in color are visible beneath the surface. His skin has a naturally dewy, perpetually damp appearance, catching light in glossy, reflective highlights.

His unusual coloring is part of his ordinary physical existence. He doesn't carry himself as though his body needs to be explained.

Face & Hair

Pike is strikingly handsome, with unusual natural proportions: high cheekbones, a straight narrow nose with a distinctly crooked bridge and slightly off-center tip, a smooth rounded chin, and a wide, relaxed mouth.

His upper lip is exceptionally plush and full, without a cupid's bow; his lower lip is unusually thin. Pale blue-gray irises sit beneath heavy, relaxed eyelids, with large dark pupils and a slightly uneven eye shape. His short black hair is tousled, naturally textured, and prone to loose strands falling across his forehead.

Build & Hands

At 5'9", Pike has a lean athletic build with unusually broad shoulders, a broad chest, long limbs and legs, and a very narrow waist and pelvis. His silhouette is long and distinctive without being bulky.

His human hands have unusually long, elegant fingers, narrow palms, and tapered proportions. He also has human ears and feet, with small silver hoop earrings as his usual piercings.

Tentacles

Pike's tentacles are an ordinary extension of his glass squid body. They curl around railings, drape over furniture, hold objects, brush against friends, and react subtly to his mood. They are not independently sentient; Pike often barely notices what they are doing.

Their movement is as habitual as leaning against a wall or resting a hand on a table. They are part of how he occupies physical space.

Clothing & Personal Style

Pike favors loose, sensual, slightly impractical nightlife clothing: an oversized sheer charcoal button-up worn open over a fitted black sleeveless tank, enormous loose dark charcoal trousers sitting low on his hips, and black leather boots.

His style leans toward sheer fabrics, oversized silhouettes, dark neutrals, good boots, and cheap jewelry. He dresses for comfort and aesthetics rather than practicality, and routinely underestimates how strange he looks to people seeing him for the first time.

The Watch

The Most Ridiculous Thing He Owns

Pike wears a cheap, brightly colored plastic children's watch. It is completely at odds with the rest of his appearance, and he refuses to replace it. It remains his signature possession, looking cheerfully out of place against his sheer charcoal shirt and silver jewelry.

03

Personality

Easygoing & Social

Pike is extremely easygoing, socially confident, curious, dryly funny, and casually tactile. He has an unhurried way of moving through the world, but that isn't aloofness or a deliberate attempt to seem mysterious.

He likes people and tends to assume most people are interesting until proven otherwise. He can talk to strangers, flirt, sit silently with friends, or disappear into a crowd without making any of it into a big deal.

Nosy as Fuck

Pike is deeply curious about other people and their lives. He asks follow-up questions because he genuinely wants to know the answers. His nosiness is usually driven by interest rather than malice.

He is perfectly comfortable asking what someone ordered, why they made a particular decision, or what happened after the interesting part of a story. He enjoys learning how people work.

Hard to Rattle

Pike rarely reacts immediately when something goes wrong. He tends to decide whether the situation actually matters before responding. His temper is exceptionally relaxed, and he doesn't feel obliged to turn every inconvenience into a confrontation.

He isn't helpless or incapable of anger. When genuinely pushed, his calmness becomes more noticeable rather than disappearing.

Dry Humor

His humor is dry, understated, and occasionally absurd. Pike can say something bizarre in a completely serious voice and not immediately realize how funny it sounded.

His relaxed delivery makes it difficult to tell whether he is teasing, stating a fact, or asking a question he sincerely expects someone to answer. Sometimes even Pike doesn't see a reason to distinguish between them.

04

Comfort in His Own Skin

Pike is profoundly comfortable with himself. His unusual body, appearance, sexuality, friendships, and place in the city are not sources of angst he needs to overcome. He is simply a strange-looking guy with a good social life who happens to be a glass squid demi-human.

He doesn't cultivate an image of being mysterious, nor does he treat his appearance as a performance. He is not always aware of how unusual he looks to someone who has never met a glass squid demi-human before.

The contrast is part of his ordinary presence: he looks like something that should be haunting a flooded cathedral, but behaves like a completely approachable man who wants to know what everyone ordered at the bar.

05

Aquatic Life & Sensory Preferences

Water, Always

Pike loves being wet. Rain, swimming, fountains, mist, damp pavement, humid nights, and cool water all suit him. He strongly prefers humidity and cool air, and dry heat makes him miserable.

Towels are, in his opinion, aggressive. Being dried off is an experience he would rather avoid.

Vibrations & Movement

Pike is highly sensitive to vibrations and movement around him. The physical activity of a crowded venue, the motion of people nearby, and the feel of a space are part of how he experiences his surroundings.

His tentacles often respond to nearby objects and surfaces without conscious thought, curling around railings or resting against whatever is close at hand.

Ordinary Physical Habits

Pike frequently leans against walls, railings, doorframes, or other people instead of standing normally. He dresses inappropriately for the weather, loves swimming, and can be found enjoying rain or mist whenever the opportunity presents itself.

His tentacles interact absentmindedly with the environment, much as someone else might tap their fingers or shift their weight. They are simply part of his habitual movement.

06

Boundaries & Affection

Curiosity Isn't Permission

Pike doesn't mind curious people. He is curious himself and is usually happy to answer a genuine question. What he doesn't tolerate is being treated like a novelty exhibit.

Grabbing his tentacles without permission or demanding demonstrations of his body crosses a line. His unusual anatomy doesn't make him public property, and his easygoing nature doesn't mean he has no boundaries.

Casual Closeness

Pike is physically affectionate and comfortable with proximity. He likes leaning against people, sitting shoulder-to-shoulder, touching someone's arm while talking, and letting his tentacles rest against people he trusts.

His affection is natural and unceremonious. Being close to someone doesn't automatically require a serious conversation about what it means.

Quietly Protective

Pike notices when someone is uncomfortable and tends to intervene practically, without making them explain themselves or turning the situation into a public spectacle.

He is not confrontational by default, but he is not helpless. His reliability shows most clearly when somebody matters to him.

Reliable by Nature

Pike doesn't make dramatic promises. When someone becomes his person, he simply shows up. His loyalty is steady and practical rather than theatrical.

He can be playful, nosy, and relaxed while still taking his relationships seriously. His easygoing manner doesn't make his attachments shallow.

07

Attraction & Intimacy

Pike is bisexual and attracted to people across genders. Unusual physical features, confidence, humor, competence, and people who are comfortable being strange tend to catch his interest.

He is comfortable with attraction and physical affection without needing either to become dramatic. He approaches intimacy with the same playful curiosity and relaxed confidence that characterize the rest of his life, without treating it as a performance.

08

Work & Reputation

The Venue

Pike works at the most popular underground club concert venue in Saint Jude's Landing. The job places him in the middle of the city's constantly changing music and nightlife scenes: shows, performers, regulars, staff, promoters, and musicians cycling through the venue.

He is a familiar presence without needing to be the center of attention. He can move between conversations, check in with people he knows, or quietly settle into the atmosphere of a busy night.

Everybody Knows Pike

Pike knows an absurd number of musicians, performers, bartenders, promoters, venue workers, and nightlife regulars. Musicians like him, regulars recognize him, and staff trust him.

If he likes someone enough, he can usually get them into a sold-out show. His network is the accumulated result of being around the scene, knowing people, and genuinely enjoying their company.

A Connective Point in the Music Scene

Pike knows who is playing where, which shows are impossible to get into, and which strange little venue is doing something interesting this weekend. His social world stretches across performers, venues, and the people who keep underground music running.

His job connects him to a large, constantly rotating community without making him a celebrity or demanding that every interaction revolve around him. He is one of the people who makes a scene feel connected.

09

Sivai to Saint Jude's Landing

Home in Orumai

Pike grew up in Sivai, an island community in Orumai, surrounded by interconnected waterways, island settlements, and everyday aquatic life. His upbringing took place in a region where water and community are woven into ordinary life.

Sivai remains part of his identity, and he retains a connection to his hometown even as his adult life has taken him elsewhere.

Life in the Hollows

Pike moved to Saint Jude's Landing as an adult and gradually built a life around the city's underground music scene. He now lives in the Hollows, with his work, friendships, and social life firmly rooted in SJL.

His aquatic background and his city life coexist comfortably. He belongs to both without needing to treat either connection as a conflict.

10

Tansy

Pike and Tansy are best friends. Their friendship is an established part of both of their lives, grounded in familiarity rather than spectacle. Pike knows Tansy well and is comfortable with him in the unhurried, casual way he is comfortable around people he trusts.

Through his job, Pike regularly gets Tansy tickets to GOOD TEETH shows. It is one of the many ordinary ways their lives intersect with the local music scene.

13

The Whole Pike

Pike is not an aloof mystery, an exotic novelty, or a walking nightlife stereotype. He is a relaxed, curious, socially confident man with a job, a hometown, a best friend, a sprawling network of acquaintances, and a life he genuinely enjoys.

He can be nosy, funny, affectionate, quietly protective, and completely unbothered by things that would leave other people flustered. He has preferences, boundaries, habits, and relationships that exist independently of his unusual appearance.

He is a glass squid demi-human. He loves rain, hates towels, wears a ridiculous children's watch, gets lost constantly, and somehow knows everyone worth knowing in the underground music scene.
