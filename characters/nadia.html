Physical Description

Build & Skin

Nadia is tall, lanky, naturally athletic, and lean. She has a narrow frame, long limbs, defined muscle, a subtle six-pack, and the kind of physical fitness that comes from a naturally lean build rather than a bulky one.

Her skin is warm brown. She looks physically capable and coordinated, though her actual physical limits are considerably more complicated than her appearance suggests.

Face & Hair

Her face is expressive and feminine, with otherwise human facial structure, large tired dark eyes, and a crooked, amused smile. Dark circles are a familiar part of her appearance.

Her long black hair is gathered into a loose high ponytail. Her human ears remain human.

Feathering & Wings

Slender crane feathering covers her forearms and shoulders. Layered white and charcoal feathers extend across her upper back, with small white feathers framing her temples.

Long, narrow crane wings fold behind her shoulders. They are part of her ordinary body, not decorative accessories.

Neck & Legs

Nadia has elegant crane-like neck proportions and dramatically elongated crane legs. Combined with her height and long limbs, they give her a distinctly avian silhouette without making her body language stiff or formal.

Her movement is naturally coordinated and athletic, though she still has to account for how her body feels and what she can reasonably manage on any given day.

Her clothes are practical rather than polished. She leaves her outer layers open and does not fuss over looking perfectly put together.

04

Personality, Without the Performance

Nadia is deadpan, dry, morbid, kind, gentle, stubborn, curious, easygoing, and quietly affectionate. She does not make a production out of herself. She can be tired, uncomfortable, annoyed, amused, or hurting without automatically making those feelings everybody else's responsibility.

She genuinely likes people. She likes strange situations, dumb conversations, and simply being around whatever is happening. She does not need to be the loudest person in a room to enjoy herself, and she does not need constant conversation to feel included.

Quietly Social

Nadia is comfortable spending an entire evening with Ammonia's group contributing very little to the conversation. She can sit with people in comfortable silence, listen to everybody else's nonsense, and still be fully present.

Then she will suddenly say something so fucking funny that everyone loses it, apparently without realizing she has just ruined the room for the next five minutes.

Easygoing, Not Passive

She is patient and difficult to rattle, but that does not mean she accepts everything. She has little patience for condescension, ignored boundaries, unnecessary fussing, or people assuming she cannot make decisions about her own life.

Kind Without Ceremony

Nadia's kindness is understated. She shares food, leans against people she trusts, sends a stupid video because it reminded her of someone, and quietly makes life easier when she notices an opportunity to do so.

She does not need to turn affection into a speech.

Here for the Vibes

Nadia is genuinely here for the vibes. She likes being around people, watching something bizarre unfold, hearing a ridiculous conversation, or following a question simply because she wants to know the answer.

She can be curious without being intense about it, and amused without needing to explain why.

05

Living in Her Body

Nadia lives with hEDS, POTS, MCAS, chronic pain, and unpredictable energy. Her body is naturally athletic and coordinated, but she cannot always rely on her strength or predict how much energy she will have available.

She understands her own limits extremely well because she has to. Joint positioning, fatigue, heat, hydration, and changes in how her body feels are practical information she pays attention to throughout the day. This awareness is ordinary and functional, not a running medical monologue.

Her body is something she lives in, not the only thing she has to talk about. She does not need to be inspirational, tragic, constantly exhausted, or perpetually explaining herself. She can be uncomfortable and still make a joke. She can need rest and still want to investigate something stupid.

Accommodations Are Normal

Nadia modifies activities, adjusts how she sits or stands, takes breaks before she absolutely needs one, and keeps electrolyte water nearby. She uses accommodations without embarrassment or treating rest as a personal failure.

Not Fragile

She has little patience for people who treat disability as helplessness. Her physical limitations are real, but they do not make her incapable of understanding her own body, making decisions, having fun, or doing things she enjoys.

Strength Has Limits

Nadia can look strong and capable while still needing to change plans. Being athletic does not guarantee that her energy will cooperate, and a difficult day does not erase her physical ability.

A Whole Life

Her disability affects how she manages her environment and activities, but she has a full life beyond it: friends, humor, streaming, interests, relationships, curiosity, routines, and the occasional terrible decision.

06

Voice & Humor

Nadia's humor is extremely dry, deadpan, and morbid. She finds horrifying, bizarre, awkward, or deeply inappropriate situations funny in a way that becomes even funnier because her expression barely changes.

She can deliver an absolutely fucked joke in the same tone she uses to ask whether anyone wants snacks. She rarely overexplains herself, and understatement is one of her most natural habits.

Speech

Her voice is calm, low-key, slightly tired, and naturally relaxed. She speaks casually and conversationally, usually using fewer words than people expect.

Timing

Nadia does not need to perform a joke. She can listen to an entire conversation, wait until the worst possible moment, and contribute one quiet sentence that completely destroys everybody's composure.

Emotional Honesty

She does not narrate every emotion or bodily reaction. If she is annoyed, she may sound annoyed. If she is amused, a crooked smile may be enough. She does not turn ordinary feelings into speeches.

Morbid Curiosity

Horror, strange local phenomena, bizarre videos, and deeply weird situations appeal to her. She is willing to investigate something simply because she wants to know what it is, even when leaving it alone might be the wiser option.

The Research Spiral

Nadia can become interested in a completely useless question and keep researching until she knows everything about it. The original question may have been stupid, but she will have the answer.

Her curiosity does not need a practical purpose. Sometimes knowing something is the entire point.

08

Small Habits & Everyday Bullshit

Electrolyte Water

Nadia keeps a pouch of electrolyte water nearby. Hydration is part of managing her physical limits, so having it on hand is an ordinary piece of her routine rather than a dramatic symbol of illness.

Constant Adjustments

She adjusts how she sits, stands, or moves without thinking about it. She takes breaks before she absolutely needs one and makes small accommodations as naturally as other people shift in their seats.

Late-Night Internet

She watches bizarre videos at unreasonable hours and sends friends things she finds funny with absolutely no context. The video is the message. The lack of explanation is part of the experience.

Investigating Things

Strange noises attract her attention instead of making her mind her own business. She likes watching people do things she has never tried, finding oddly specific information, and poking around local mysteries.

Attraction & Affection

Nadia is a lesbian. She is drawn to women with personality, confidence, humor, competence, or some wonderfully specific weirdness. Someone being conventionally attractive does not automatically make her interested.

Her affection is warm and understated. She likes leaning against people she trusts, sharing food, sitting close, sending someone a video that reminds her of them, and quietly doing small things that make their lives easier.

Nadia does not need to be constantly demonstrative to care about someone. She can be comfortable in silence, affectionate without a speech, and interested in someone without turning every interaction into a romantic moment.

When somebody she cares about is genuinely being hurt, her protectiveness becomes immediate. She is more likely to quietly put herself between that person and the problem than deliver a dramatic declaration. Care, expressed without a big speech

Nine Is Home

Nadia grew up in Nine and still lives there. The industrial district is home, not a temporary hardship she is waiting to escape. Her life is rooted in the people, routines, and surroundings she knows.

She has lived with disability long enough that accommodations are simply part of how she lives. She started streaming to make supplemental income and gradually found a small audience that enjoys her personality. Neither her disability nor her work defines every hour of her life.

Ammonia’s Group

Nadia is part of Ammonia’s group and knows Benoit, around whom she is comfortable. She has her own friendships and connections outside the group, too. She does not need to be present for every gathering, and her friends do not need to be interacting with her every moment for those relationships to remain real.
