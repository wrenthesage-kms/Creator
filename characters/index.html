Everybody's got a life here.

A growing directory of the people who live on Creator, from Saint Jude's Landing regulars and strange neighborhood fixtures to musicians whose songs have taken over the planet. Humans, demi-humans, and monsters share cities, homes, workplaces, friendships, complicated histories, and ordinary daily life. No two people fit into quite the same box, and most of them have something going on.
