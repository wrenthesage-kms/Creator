GOOD TEETH are a touring band with a sound that crosses electronic production, hyperpop-adjacent hooks, emo, post-hardcore, technical guitar, melodic songwriting, and full-force hardcore. The five members bring their own influences, habits, and personalities to the music.

That's the thing about Creator.

These aren't just species, jobs, aesthetics, or funny little neighborhood anecdotes. They're people with routines, relationships, bad ideas, favorite places, private histories, and lives that keep moving when nobody's looking. There's always somebody else around the corner.
