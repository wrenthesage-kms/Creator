Appearance

✿

Build & presence

Bell is tall and powerfully built, with substantial muscularity and a confident, feminine presence. Her strength is visible without swallowing the rest of her appearance. She can look glamorous, imposing, elegant, or ridiculous depending on the situation—and sometimes manage all four at once.

Face & expression

She has an attractive, expressive face capable of an astonishingly soft, goofy, lovestruck expression. Bell can maintain a perfectly coherent conversation while her expression gives away every thought she was trying to keep to herself.

Hair & eyes

Bell has blonde hair and blue eyes. Hair is not merely something she happens to work with; it is one of her genuine interests and a central part of her personal beauty routines. She takes her appearance seriously without needing to take herself seriously every second.

Secretary-bird features

Her ears are human. Subtle secretary-bird feathering covers her neck and décolletage, adding a distinctive avian detail to an otherwise polished, glamorous presentation. Her hands and feet are human.

Vintage glamour

Bell favors high-end clothing with a vintage-glamorous, polished feminine aesthetic. Her style reflects genuine care for fashion, beauty, and presentation. She looks put together because she enjoys the process and knows what she likes—not because she is trying to prove she belongs in an expensive room.

02

Personality

♡

Confident & self-assured

Bell knows who she is. She can hold eye contact, stand her ground, express an opinion, and handle a difficult conversation without folding under somebody else's confidence. She does not automatically surrender the room to a woman she finds attractive.

Warm & socially capable

She is comfortable talking to people, making conversation, and expressing herself. Bell can be friendly without being a pushover and assertive without being needlessly hostile. She has a personality outside her romantic interests and knows how to use it.

Hopelessly expressive

When Bell finds a woman attractive, her face can betray her so completely that the situation becomes funny. She might be making a strong argument while looking as though she has just witnessed the most beautiful thing in the universe. She knows this happens, and she can laugh at herself.

Self-aware & goofy

Bell has enough self-awareness to recognize when she's being ridiculous. That doesn't necessarily stop her. She can own the cheesiness, laugh at herself, and keep right on talking instead of becoming embarrassed into silence.

More than the punchline

Her lovestruck expression is a funny contradiction, not her entire personality. Bell has opinions, boundaries, friendships, a career, a home, daily routines, interests, and a full life. She is allowed to be competent and ridiculous, glamorous and goofy, strong and affectionate without any of those qualities canceling out the others.

Attraction & Boundaries

♡

Attracted, not incapacitated

Bell becomes visibly lovestruck around women she finds attractive. She does not necessarily become quiet, stammering, or tongue-tied. She can remain articulate, conversational, opinionated, and perfectly capable of making her point while looking completely enchanted.

Compliments come naturally

She might be discussing politics, hair, dinner, or somebody's terrible decision-making and casually slip in an earnest compliment about how beautiful the other woman is. Bell does not need every conversation to become a seduction, but she isn't going to pretend she failed to notice a gorgeous woman, either.

She will hold her ground

If another woman invades her space, Bell can meet her gaze and refuse to back down. She can challenge someone, disagree, tease, or tell them to move while her expression makes it abundantly clear that she finds them attractive. Confidence and attraction can coexist.

Boundaries remain boundaries

Being attracted to somebody does not mean Bell automatically welcomes every advance or accepts every behavior. She is capable of setting limits, saying no, and responding when somebody pushes too far. Her attraction never makes her passive or removes her agency.

The contradiction is the point.

Bell can be a confident, self-assured woman who refuses to be pushed around and an embarrassing lovestruck dork who cannot hide how pretty she thinks somebody is. She doesn't need to choose between those things. The woman can be gorgeous, irritating, wrong, pushy, fascinating, and still worth looking at—and Bell is allowed to have complicated reactions to that.

06

Death, Decay & Decomposition

☾

Bell is not afraid of death. She is perfectly capable of handling a situation in which someone has died, looking at what needs to be done, and dealing with the practical reality of the situation.

What unsettles her is decomposition: the physical transition from a living body into a carcass that is beginning to rot. Visible decay, insects moving through remains, and the unmistakable reality of flesh deteriorating are what make her uncomfortable.

She doesn't become hysterical or prissy about it. She can look, understand, and do what needs to be done. The difficulty is that she cannot completely separate the remains from the living person or creature they used to be. She sees both at once: what is there now, and the living thing that existed before.

It's the transformation, not death itself.

Bell's discomfort comes from the raw physical reality of decay. She can remain composed and practical while still finding it deeply unpleasant. Being capable of dealing with something does not mean it fails to affect her.
