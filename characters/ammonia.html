02 / Under the surface

Personality

Quiet when she has nothing to say. Entirely capable of making herself a problem.

Ammonia is observant, physically intuitive, blunt, comfortable with herself, independent, curious, and impulsive.

She understands social rules perfectly well. She simply does not always care about following them.

She watches first. Listens. Smells things. Notices movement, vibration, structural changes, unfamiliar sounds, and tiny environmental details other people overlook. When something matters to her, her curiosity can become intense, unwavering focus.

Her humor is natural rather than performed.

When angry, she may shove, bite, scratch, throw things, or leave. Her aggression is physical and immediate rather than theatrical.
