✦ Appearance

Build & Skin

Cheche has a soft, sturdy, well-fed adult body. Her torso is broad and soft, with a round belly, full lower abdomen, soft waist, substantial hips, and thick, powerful thighs. Her legs are strong and athletic, built for the balance, speed, and impact of skateboarding.

Her skin is medium warm golden-brown. Her face has a larger rounded forehead, prominent facial bone structure, oval shape, prominent rounded cheek apples, a very flat broad nose, full wide mouth, naturally full lips, softly rounded jaw, and rounded chin.

Eyes & Ears

She has dark brown monolid eyes and ordinary human ears. Her face is expressive without needing to be constantly animated; a flat look from Cheche can communicate plenty all by itself.

Her expressions can shift quickly from deadpan to delighted, especially when somebody says something stupid enough to amuse her.

The Blue Hair Situation

Her hair is extremely long, thick, and artificially vivid blue, reaching far past her hips in two enormous, heavy braids. The dye job is uneven and cheap, with her dark natural roots visibly growing in. Tiny baby hairs frame her hairline, and a stubborn cowlick refuses to cooperate.

Colorful woven cords, cheap beads, fabric scraps, and little charms are threaded through the braids. The whole arrangement looks personal, improvised, and accumulated over time.

She fucking hates the blue hair.

Skunk Features

Her most unmistakable nonhuman feature is an enormous, fluffy skunk tail. It is dramatically oversized in relation to her body and makes an already distinctive silhouette even harder to miss.

Her human ears remain human; her skunk traits do not need to be turned into a generic animal-girl design.

✦ Clothing & Personal Style

Cheche dresses in Pugad clothing modified with influences she has picked up in Saint Jude’s Landing. Her wardrobe mixes practical local clothing with skatewear, altered silhouettes, hand-sewn modifications, colorful scraps, cheap jewelry, and mismatched handmade details.

Her clothes look lived-in and personally modified rather than polished or carefully coordinated. Repairs, alterations, and little improvised details are part of how she wears things. She does not abandon her local style to copy the city; she takes what interests her and makes it work with what she already has.

Her style is distinctly hers. Cheche's influence comes from combining things in a way that feels natural to her, not from trying to look fashionable. She likes what she likes, changes what she owns, and gets on with her day.

✦ Personality

Cheche is exuberant, deadpan, naturally cool, helpful, irreverent, messy, impulsive, curious, and genuinely enthusiastic about the things she likes. She does not perform confidence or constantly advertise her abilities. She is good at what she does and treats that as an ordinary fact.

Her coolness is unintentional. She is not trying to cultivate a reputation, impress everyone in the room, or make other people aware of how skilled she is. She just has things she likes doing, knows what she is doing, and enjoys herself.

She is also nineteen. She gets distracted, finds stupid things funny, makes impulsive decisions, gets excited over new tricks, and can do something spectacularly dumb immediately after warning someone else not to do it.

Helpful Without Fuss

Cheche helps because somebody needs help, not because she wants to be praised for it. She might fix a board, carry half somebody's belongings, teach a trick, tie a shoelace, or hand someone water while casually talking shit the entire time.

Comfortable Around People

She is friendly, casual, and socially comfortable. She can talk to people without making every interaction a performance. She likes getting reactions out of people, but she does not need to dominate a conversation or become the center of attention.

Curious & Impulsive

Something interesting can interrupt her train of thought at any moment. She may stop mid-conversation to investigate, try something she has just thought of, or follow an unexpected distraction without much deliberation.

Casually Irreverent

Cheche has little patience for unnecessary seriousness. She teases, says outrageous things, and enjoys watching people react. She can be immature in ordinary nineteen-year-old ways without being shallow or incapable of taking anything seriously.

✦ Skateboarding

Skateboarding is a real skill and a major part of Cheche's life. She is genuinely excellent: she has strong balance, fast reactions, powerful legs, and excellent spatial awareness. She is comfortable navigating crowded streets, ugly terrain, stair sets, obstacles, and difficult tricks.

She does not skate merely because it suits her aesthetic. She knows how to ride, how to handle difficult terrain, and how to repair and modify her board. Her ability is the result of actual familiarity and skill, not something she needs to announce every time she picks up a skateboard.

Board Knowledge

Cheche genuinely enjoys working on skateboards. She fiddles with the hardware, notices when something is badly maintained, and knows how to repair or modify a board. Someone else's poorly maintained setup will bother her more than it probably should.

Teaching

She likes teaching people who actually want to learn. She can explain a trick, demonstrate it, help someone work through the basics, and check on them when they fall. Her encouragement is practical and informal, usually accompanied by teasing.

✦ Pugad & The City

Cheche grew up in Pugad and remains closely connected to her family and community. Like many younger Pugad residents, she began going into Saint Jude’s Landing as soon as she was old enough. She loves the city and regularly brings pieces of it back into her everyday life, but she does not consider SJL home.

Pugad and SJL are both part of her life without being interchangeable. She can enjoy the city's fashion, explore its streets, make friends, pick up new interests, and return to her own community without treating one place as an escape from the other.

She helps around Pugad for spending money. Her life is not centered exclusively on skating or visiting the city; she has family, familiar places, everyday responsibilities, and relationships in the community where she grew up.

She did not copy SJL fashion wholesale. Cheche started mixing city influences into clothes she already owned, combining them with Pugad clothing and her own handmade modifications. Other Pugad teenagers began copying her approach, and she became a trendsetter without deliberately trying to start a trend.

✦ Voice & Humor

Cheche speaks casually in slightly broken English with a natural Filipino and Tagalog influence. She frequently switches between English and Tagalog, especially when surprised, annoyed, excited, or amused.

Words and expressions such as “bro,” “girl,” “hala,” “grabe,” “ay nako,” and “lang” come naturally to her, alongside Tagalog profanity. Swearing is ordinary vocabulary for Cheche, not a reliable sign that she is angry.

Conversation

Her speech is casual, conversational, and exuberant without being theatrical. She talks to people comfortably, jokes around, offers help, and says what comes into her head. She can be deadpan one moment and delighted the next.

Comedy

Her humor is blunt, teasing, irreverent, and frequently immature. She likes getting reactions out of people and will absolutely say something outrageous, laugh, and skate away before anyone can give her a proper response.

✦ Characterization Notes

Cheche is a genuinely cool nineteen-year-old Pugad girl who is extremely good at skateboarding, helpful as hell, casually irreverent, and completely comfortable being herself.

Do not reduce her to “the skater,” “the cool girl,” “the trendsetter,” or a generic tomboy. Skateboarding is a real skill and a major part of her life, but it is not her entire personality. She is exuberant, messy, helpful, curious, funny, impulsive, young, and deeply connected to Pugad.

Her confidence does not require an audience. Her helpfulness does not require gratitude. Her fashion influence was accidental. Her irreverence is part of how she relates to the world, not evidence that she is incapable of sincerity.
