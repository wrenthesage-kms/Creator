Small Man / Big Feelings

RENGA

Small man. Large tail. Excellent brain.

A lanky, sleepy-looking ringtail demi-human with a gentle nature, a sharp little brain, and absolutely no idea how much his tail gives away. Renga is quiet without being shy, clever without showing off, and far more observant than his permanently tired expression suggests. He isn't trying to be cute. Unfortunately for him, he is.

01

Appearance

Face & Expression

Renga has warm human skin and a soft, youthful, distinctly baby-faced appearance. His narrow, tired monolid eyes are pale brown. He often looks mildly unimpressed, half-asleep, or quietly amused, with expressions that shift just enough to reveal that he has been paying attention the entire time.

His ears are completely human. He has no animal ears.

Hair

Short, tousled rusty orange-brown hair with a naturally sun-faded quality. It is uneven and messy, with pieces sticking out wherever they please. The effect is less deliberate styling than someone waking up, running a hand through his hair, and deciding that was probably enough.

Build & Movement

Small and lightly built, with narrow shoulders, a slim waist, long limbs for his height, and a delicate-looking frame. His clothes hang loosely rather than emphasizing his shape. He lacks heavy muscle definition but has wiry, functional strength beneath his soft appearance.

His skin feels warm and soft, particularly around his ribs and stomach. His hips are narrow, his thighs long and lean, and the fur of his tail extends down to the base of his spine.

Everyday Clothes

An oversized dusty-cream T-shirt and baggy, faded-blue denim pants made from Behemoth hide. The pants sit unusually high, nearly reaching his armpits. They are loose, worn, practical, and thoroughly lived-in.

A single hot-magenta carabiner is attached high on the pants, integrated into the garment rather than hanging from an ordinary belt loop. It is a bright little interruption in an otherwise faded outfit.

The Ringtail

Renga's most conspicuous nonhuman feature is his enormous fluffy ringtail, marked with distinct natural rings. It is large and expressive in proportion to his body, moving with the casual independence of another limb. It curls around objects, wraps around his legs, coils close to him when he is emotional, and sometimes communicates feelings he would rather keep to himself.

The tail is part of his everyday physical life, not a decorative accessory. Renga is completely accustomed to it. Other people may find it harder to ignore, especially when it starts making his feelings obvious before he has said a word.

02

The Tail Has Its Own Agenda

Renga can keep his face almost perfectly still while his tail carries on having an entire emotional experience. He does not always notice what it is doing until someone points it out. Then he gets embarrassed and attempts to make it stop. This is not a particularly reliable solution.

Comfortable His tail curls loosely around his legs or settles wherever he has made himself comfortable.
Nervous It becomes restless, shifting and moving even while he stays outwardly composed.
Embarrassed It may coil tightly around him, making his attempt to look unaffected rather unconvincing.
Sleepy It drapes across whoever happens to be closest, as if this is a perfectly ordinary arrangement.
Genuinely Upset His face may remain almost motionless, but his tail becomes intensely expressive. The contrast makes his emotional state difficult to miss for anyone who knows what to look for.
Hands off unless invited. Renga dislikes people grabbing his tail without permission. He is comfortable with familiar physical affection, including having his tail absentmindedly touched by someone he trusts, but familiarity is not blanket permission for strangers to handle him or his belongings.
03

Personality

Quiet, Not Shy

Renga tends to sit back and watch before deciding whether he wants to participate. He doesn't feel compelled to fill every silence or announce every observation. When he speaks, he usually has an actual reason.

He is gentle by default, but that gentleness should not be confused with delicacy, helplessness, or an inability to stand his ground.

Deadpan Humor

His sense of humor is sleepy, understated, and often ridiculous. Renga can deliver something absurd in exactly the same tone he would use to ask somebody to pass him a drink. The expression rarely warns anyone that a joke is coming.

Gentle & Stubborn

Renga prefers solutions that create the least amount of noise. He doesn't enjoy unnecessary confrontation and is often content to let a situation resolve without making himself its center.

When something genuinely matters to him, however, he can be surprisingly stubborn. Quiet does not mean compliant.

More Transparent Than He Thinks

Renga does not always realize how much he reveals through small movements, expressions, and habits. His face might give very little away, but his tail is another matter entirely. He is often less mysterious to other people than he believes himself to be.

04

A Sharp Little Brain

Pattern Recognition

Renga is good at noticing mechanical problems, patterns, inconsistencies, shortcuts, and details other people overlook. He understands systems especially well after he has had time to observe how they work.

He is clever without being showy. He doesn't need an audience for his conclusions, and he rarely feels the urge to prove that he knows something.

Quiet Problem-Solving

Sometimes he will stare at a problem for several minutes, make one small adjustment, and solve the whole thing. If somebody asks how he figured it out, he may genuinely answer, “I dunno.”

He does know. He just doesn't always know how to explain the process that got him there.

05

GOOD TEETH: Electronic Architecture

Renga is GOOD TEETH's synth and keyboard player. His work contributes electronic architecture, unusual textures, strange melodies, loops, effects, atmospheric layers, bright hooks, distorted electronics, and rhythms that can make a song feel playful, futuristic, melancholy, abrasive, or wonderfully bizarre.

Electronic elements are not decoration added on top of the band's sound. They are part of how GOOD TEETH builds its music, colliding with technical guitar, bass, drums, vocals, and sudden changes in intensity. Renga's contribution can be bright and catchy one moment, then strange and atmospheric the next.

The band is not organized around a designated cute member, mascot, or baby. Renga is a twenty-four-year-old musician with his own opinions, habits, irritations, desires, friendships, and private jokes. His small frame and baby face are simply part of what he looks like.

06

Life on Tour

Sleep Comes Easily

Renga can sleep almost anywhere when he is tired enough. Given the opportunity, he curls up and gets comfortable, often with his tail wrapped around his legs. Quiet mornings and long naps are among his favorite things.

He Notices Things

He develops very specific preferences about where his belongings go, remembers which convenience stores have good food, and notices strange noises in hotels. Unfamiliar places tend to become familiar to him surprisingly quickly.

The Missing Object Problem

Renga is often the person who notices that somebody has forgotten something important. He may not announce it, make a fuss, or explain how he realized it was missing. He simply appears beside the person holding the object they need.

His attention is quiet and practical. He notices what is happening, remembers useful details, and acts when he has a reason to.

The Whole Renga

Renga is a sleepy little bastard with a good heart. He is clever without being showy, gentle without being passive, strange without trying to be strange, and affectionate without making a production out of affection.

He can be quietly stubborn, observant, practical, funny, and surprisingly quick to understand a problem. He likes warm food, strange machines, long naps, familiar company, and the comfort of not having to talk just to prove he is there.

His tail says everything he refuses to say. He usually notices a little too late.
